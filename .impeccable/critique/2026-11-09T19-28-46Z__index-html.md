## Priority issues (P0–P1)

1. **P0 — Hero hierarchy weak vs product job**  
   Visible H1 is `visually-hidden`; logo panel dominates. Primary visitor job (understand → open bot) needs one visible headline, short support, one dominant Telegram CTA.

2. **P0 — Competing first actions**  
   Header «КАБІНЕТ» competes with Telegram CTA. Cabinet is for returning coaches; marketing should demote it relative to bot entry.

3. **P1 — Navigation legibility / density**  
   Detector: nav labels ~10.88px (below 11px floor). Mobile uses dense 3×2 grid without collapse. Enlarge functional text and simplify narrow viewports.

4. **P1 — Contrast edge cases**  
   Detector advisory: `#e30613` on `#f4f4f4` ≈ 4.4:1 (need 4.5:1 for small text). Adjust red-on-light usages for AA.

5. **P1 — Cramped containers**  
   Detector: section / cabinet-card padding flush to edges. Add inset (12–16px) inside bordered/filled containers.

## Persona red flags

- **Coach evaluating:** “Кабінет” may look like the product before they understand the bot is the product.
